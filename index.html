Dear friend,

In 2009 Grantham Gingerbread, England's oldest biscuit, had fallen out of production. I started baking it again with no equipment and no know how, rolling every biscuit by hand. Over sixteen years it found its way into Selfridges, John Lewis, The National Trust and around 6,500 stores, and in 2025 it gave work to 50 local people.

Grantham Gingerbread belongs to Grantham. So I am gifting my knowledge, know how and recipes to the town, and setting up The 1740 Foundation so that it is baked in Grantham, by people from Grantham, for the benefit of the whole community.

The Foundation will create real jobs and training for people who face barriers to work, including ex service personnel, neurodivergent and disabled people, people who have been homeless and ex offenders, and it will support enterprise and apprenticeships across the town.

To unlock the grant funding that makes this possible, funders need to see that Grantham wants it. That is where you come in. Adding your name below takes a minute, and every name makes our case stronger. If you can give a little time or a skill, please tell us that too.

Thank you for being part of the next chapter.

Alastair Hawken
Founder, The 1740 Foundation
